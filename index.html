About me

Practical understanding from software to IT infrastructure

I study Information Technology at Oulu University of Applied Sciences, with a focus on software development. My software development experience comes from engineering studies, collaborative application projects and independent projects.

My professional background includes IT services, Microsoft 365 administration, Entra ID, Intune, user and device management, second-line technical support, documentation and infrastructure-related work.

I also have experience in electronics manufacturing, functional testing, measurement and quality assurance. This background helps me understand how software, hardware, infrastructure, information and users connect in practical technical environments.

Before IT, I spent over a decade in customer-facing roles — retail sales and category responsibility, then warehouse operations and multi-channel technical support in e-commerce. Turns out explaining to a frustrated customer why their order is late is excellent training for explaining to a frustrated user why their license didn't provision.

Current direction

I'm aiming for junior backend, full stack, automation and AI-assisted software roles — the kind where "works on my machine" doesn't count, and I get to prove it in production too.

My IT background isn't a detour on the way to software development. It's why I already know what happens after the code ships.
